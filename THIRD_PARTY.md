## LibDataBroker-1.1

Author: Tekkub. The upstream repository does not include a conventional license file. Its official project page labels it All Rights Reserved and explicitly instructs addon authors to hard-embed it and declare a `libdatabroker-1-1` tools-used relationship. LuaWorkbench follows that distribution guidance and preserves the library unchanged; it does not relicense this library under MIT or claim broader modification rights.
